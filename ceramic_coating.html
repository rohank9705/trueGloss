Ceramic Coating

What is ceramic coating?

Lock in a flawless, glass-like finish while shielding your paintwork against the elements. Our professional ceramic coatings deliver years of durable protection, expertly tailored to your vehicle's specific needs.

Every vehicle is different. Contact True Gloss to understand your options and receive a personalised recommendation.

Why Invest in a Ceramic Coating?

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied by hand to the exterior of your vehicle. It chemically bonds with the factory paint, creating a durable, semi-permanent layer of protection that wax simply cannot match.

Extreme Gloss

Enhances the reflective properties of your paint and clear coat, locking in a deep, glass-like showroom finish.

Hydrophobic Shield

Water beads up and rolls aggressively off the surface, encapsulating dirt and making routine maintenance washes effortless.

Genuine Protection

Shields your clear coat against harmful UV rays, oxidation, bird droppings, tree sap, and harsh chemical stains.

The Preparation Process

A coating is only as good as the paint beneath it. Every ceramic package includes meticulous preparation to ensure flawless bonding and maximum longevity.

  • 01
    Deep Clean & Decontamination

    A safe multi-stage wash followed by chemical iron fallout removal and physical clay bar treatment to strip embedded contaminants.

  • 02
    Machine Polishing (Paint Correction)

    Single or multi-stage machine polishing to permanently remove swirl marks, light scratches, and holograms, restoring absolute clarity.

  • 03
    Panel Wipe Down

    Stripping all remaining polish oils and residues to ensure a clinically clean, bare surface for the coating to bond to.

  • 04
    Application & Curing

    Precision application of our premium ceramic coating, carefully leveled and left to cure in a controlled environment.
